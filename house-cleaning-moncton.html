Frequently Asked Questions

House Cleaning FAQs

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

No. Refresh Cleaning Pros brings professional cleaning supplies and eco-friendly products. If you have a product you prefer for a specific surface, just let us know before your appointment.

Do you offer recurring house cleaning?

Yes. We offer weekly, bi-weekly and monthly house cleaning in Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac and Salisbury. Recurring service keeps your home consistently fresh and easier to maintain.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients provide entry instructions so we can clean while they are at work or running errands. We confirm details in advance and treat your home with care and respect.

Can I customize my home cleaning checklist?

Yes. We can focus on specific rooms, high-traffic areas, bathrooms, kitchens, floors, dusting or add extra attention where your home needs it most.

Are your cleaning products safe for kids and pets?

We use eco-friendly, non-toxic products whenever possible. Our goal is to deliver a clean home that feels healthier for families, pets and guests.
