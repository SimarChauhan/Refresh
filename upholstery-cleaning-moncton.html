Frequently Asked Questions

Upholstery Cleaning FAQs

What furniture can you clean?

We clean many sofas, sectionals, loveseats, chairs and upholstered furniture pieces. Fabric type and condition are reviewed before cleaning.

Can upholstery cleaning remove pet odors?

Professional cleaning can reduce many pet-related odors by removing hair, dander and residue. Severe or long-set odors may need additional treatment or may not fully disappear.

How long does upholstery take to dry?

Drying time depends on fabric type, ventilation, humidity and how much moisture the fabric holds. We use a controlled process to help furniture dry as efficiently as possible.

Is upholstery cleaning safe for delicate fabrics?

We inspect upholstery before cleaning and choose a method based on fabric type and condition. Some delicate or specialty fabrics may require special handling or may not be suitable for standard wet cleaning.

How often should sofas and chairs be cleaned?

Most homes benefit from upholstery cleaning every 12 to 18 months. Homes with pets, children, allergies or heavy use may prefer more frequent service.
