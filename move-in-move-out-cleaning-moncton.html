Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning in Greater Moncton

Built for Tight Timelines and Important Handoffs

Move-in and move-out cleaning is different from routine house cleaning. The space is often empty, deadlines are real and small details matter because someone is inspecting, buying, renting or moving in next.

Inspection-Ready Detail

We focus on the areas people notice during a walkthrough: appliance exteriors, cabinet fronts, sinks, mirrors, floors, baseboards, light switches, door frames and bathroom surfaces. The goal is a clean property that feels ready.

Flexible Access

Many move cleans happen while clients are packing, signing paperwork or coordinating keys. We can work from clear access instructions and confirm the checklist before arrival so the appointment stays simple.

For Rentals and Listings

Our move cleaning service helps tenants, homeowners, realtors and property managers present a space with more confidence. A cleaner property photographs better, shows better and feels more welcoming.

Frequently Asked Questions

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning FAQs

Do I need to be present during the cleaning?

No. Many clients provide access instructions so we can clean while they handle the move. We confirm expectations and contact details before the appointment.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes. Refresh Cleaning Pros brings professional supplies and equipment. If a property requires specific products, tell us during booking.

How long does move in or move out cleaning take?

Timing depends on the size of the property, condition, number of rooms and cleaning checklist. We provide guidance after learning about your space.

Do you clean rental properties?

Yes. We clean apartments, houses, rental properties and real estate listings for tenants, landlords, property managers and realtors.

What if I am not satisfied?

We stand behind our work. If something is missed, contact us promptly and we will do our best to make it right.
