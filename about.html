Our Story

Built to Raise the Standard for Cleaning in Moncton

ReFresh Cleaning Pros was created for homeowners and business owners who want more than a quick surface clean. Our goal is to provide a dependable, polished cleaning experience that feels organized from the first quote request to the final walkthrough.

As a local Moncton cleaning company, we understand the trust it takes to invite cleaners into your home, office, clinic, retail store or rental property. That is why we focus on clear communication, respectful service, professional equipment and cleaning plans that match the way each space is actually used.

We take pride in using eco-friendly, non-toxic products whenever possible to create a healthier environment for families, pets, employees and customers. Whether we are refreshing a family home, preparing a move-out, cleaning carpets or maintaining a commercial workplace, we treat every space with care and attention to detail.
