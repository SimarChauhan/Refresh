Frequently Asked Questions

Carpet Cleaning FAQs

How long does it take for carpets to dry?

Drying time depends on carpet type, airflow, humidity and room temperature. Most carpets feel dry within several hours, and we remove excess moisture to help speed up the process.

Are your carpet cleaning products safe for kids and pets?

We use family-conscious, eco-friendly products whenever possible. We can also discuss any sensitivities or product preferences before your carpet cleaning appointment.

Can you remove all stains?

Many stains improve significantly with professional treatment, but results depend on the stain type, age, carpet fiber and previous cleaning attempts. We inspect problem areas before cleaning and set clear expectations.

How often should I have my carpets cleaned?

Most homes benefit from professional carpet cleaning every 6 to 12 months. Homes with pets, kids, allergies or heavy traffic may need more frequent cleaning.

Do you move furniture?

We can discuss light furniture movement during booking. Large, fragile or heavy items should be moved before the appointment so the team can clean efficiently and safely.
