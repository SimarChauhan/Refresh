Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What cleaning products do you use?
We use eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning products that are safe for your family, pets, and the environment. All our products are professionally selected to ensure effective cleaning without harmful chemicals.
How much do your services cost?
Our pricing depends on the size of your space, type of cleaning, and frequency. We offer competitive rates and provide free quotes. Contact us for a personalized estimate.
Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?
Yes, we bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. You don't need to provide anything. Just let us know if you have any specific preferences.
How long does a typical cleaning take?
The duration depends on the size of your space and the type of cleaning. A standard residential cleaning typically takes 2-4 hours, while deep cleaning may take longer.
Are you insured?
Yes, we are fully licensed and insured. This protects both you and us in the unlikely event of any damage or accidents during service.
Do you offer regular cleaning schedules?
Absolutely! We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly cleaning schedules. We can customize a schedule that works best for you and your needs.
