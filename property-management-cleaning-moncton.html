Frequently Asked Questions

Property Management Cleaning FAQs

Useful answers for building owners, condo boards, facility teams and property managers in Greater Moncton.

Do you provide cleaning for apartment buildings?

Yes. We create recurring apartment building cleaning programs for lobbies, hallways, elevators, stairwells, laundry rooms, mail areas, amenity spaces and other shared areas. The exact checklist is based on your layout and traffic.

Can you clean multiple properties?

Yes. We can coordinate cleaning across a portfolio and discuss standardized reporting, service frequencies and points of contact while still accounting for each property's access and priorities.

Do you provide unit turnover cleaning?

Yes. Vacant-unit and move-out cleaning remains available as part of our property management service. It can support leasing, inspections, real-estate preparation and incoming residents.

Do you provide floor stripping and waxing?

Yes, for suitable resilient commercial flooring. We inspect the floor material and current finish before recommending stripping and waxing, burnishing or another floor-care approach.

Do you offer emergency cleaning services?

We can discuss urgent cleaning requests based on crew and equipment availability. Contact us directly with the property location, affected area and required timeline so we can assess the response.

Are your cleaning teams insured?

Yes. Refresh Cleaning Pros is fully insured for professional residential and commercial cleaning work.

Can cleaning schedules be customized?

Absolutely. Daily, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly and seasonal programs are available. We tailor the scope, access time, frequency and rotating detail tasks to the property.

Do you provide cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes. We arrive with the professional equipment and products required for the agreed scope. Any property-specific product, safety or access requirements should be discussed during the walkthrough.
