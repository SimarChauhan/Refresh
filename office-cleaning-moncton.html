Commercial Standards

Scheduling, Safety and Quality Control

A commercial cleaning plan should be predictable. We help business owners and managers reduce cleaning friction with clear routines, careful products and a practical communication process.

Custom Schedules

Daily, weekly, bi-weekly and custom schedules are available depending on traffic, washroom use, floor type and operating hours. We can clean before opening, after closing or during low-traffic windows.

Safety Procedures

We choose appropriate products for the environment, pay attention to wet-floor safety, handle high-touch areas carefully and respect site-specific requirements for clinics, offices and customer-facing spaces.

Quality Control

Clear checklists, recurring expectations and final review habits keep the service consistent. If priorities change, we adjust the plan so the cleaning continues to match your business.

Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Cleaning FAQs

Do you clean commercial spaces after business hours?

Yes. We offer flexible commercial cleaning times, including after-hours and recurring schedules, so cleaning does not interrupt staff, patients, customers or daily operations.

Can you create a custom cleaning plan for our business?

Yes. We customize commercial cleaning based on square footage, traffic, washrooms, break areas, flooring, workstations, industry needs and preferred frequency.

Do you clean medical facilities and dental clinics?

Yes. We can support medical and dental environments with careful cleaning for waiting areas, washrooms, staff areas and high-touch surfaces. We discuss site-specific requirements before service begins.

Do you offer floor care and janitorial service?

Yes. Floor care and janitorial upkeep can be included in a recurring commercial cleaning plan for offices, retail spaces, clinics and commercial buildings.

Are your cleaning products safe for workplaces?

We use eco-friendly products whenever possible and choose supplies appropriate for professional workspaces, clinics, retail environments and shared areas.

Do you provide all cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our team arrives with the cleaning supplies and equipment needed for the service. We can also follow any site-specific product requirements you provide.

What areas do you service?

We provide commercial cleaning in Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, Salisbury and surrounding Greater Moncton communities.
